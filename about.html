Our Story

Delhi's Most Trusted Dental Destination

Founded by Dr. Sween Kathuria (B.D.S., M.D.S., F.P.F.A. USA) and Dr. Puneet Kathuria (B.D.S., M.D.S., F.I.C.O.I. USA), our clinic has been transforming smiles since 2006.

From a single consultation room to a state-of-the-art multi-speciality dental centre, we have grown by letting results speak for themselves. NDTV named us one of the 5 Best Dental Clinics in Delhi. We are Femina Miss India's Smile Care Partner and have served over 15,000 patients from 44+ countries.

We hold a University of Louisville (USA) tie-up for sterilisation monitoring, follow ADA standards, and are OSHA compliant — the same standards upheld by the world's best dental practices.
